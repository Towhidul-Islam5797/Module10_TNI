SPRINT 1 EXERCISES

EASY
Change the damage to 25 and the cooldown to 0.5 seconds in the Inspector.
How many punches does it now take to bring the enemy from 100 to 0 health?

MEDIUM
Make a second enemy with its own Health. Stand so both are inside the red sphere and punch once.
Do both lose health? Explain why using the OverlapSphere line in DealDamage.

CHALLENGE
Add a kick on the right mouse button using Front Kick.fbx.
It needs: a second Attack-style trigger and state in ThirdPersonAnimator, a new HitFrame event on the Front Kick clip,
and a stronger hit with a longer cooldown. Reuse the existing scripts as much as you can.
