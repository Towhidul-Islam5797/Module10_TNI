SPRINT 10 EXERCISES

EASY
Make a new recipe without writing any code: 3 Coin -> 1 Gem.
Hint: Create > Crafting > Recipe, then add it to the Recipes list on the Player's
CraftingGrid. Think about it: does this recipe clash with GoldBar (4 Coin)? Why not?

MEDIUM
Add a method to CraftingGrid:
    public int CountRecipesYouCanMake()
It returns how many of the recipes could be crafted RIGHT NOW from the items in the
Inventory (ignore the grid). Log the answer every time a craft is completed.
Hint: for each recipe, check every ingredient against the inventory with a small helper
"CountInInventory(ItemData item)" that adds up the counts of all slots with that item.

CHALLENGE
Make the result count matter. Change the GoldBar recipe to give 2 Gold Bars.
1. Check that crafting adds 2 Gold Bars (it should already work: why?).
2. Uncrafting 1 Gold Bar now gives back 4 coins. That is a cheat: craft 2, uncraft 2,
   get 8 coins from 4. Fix it: only allow uncrafting when the recipe's ResultCount is 1,
   and log "Can't uncraft <name>: its recipe makes more than one".
