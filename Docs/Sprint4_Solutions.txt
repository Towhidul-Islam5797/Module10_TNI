SPRINT 4 SOLUTIONS

EASY
Enemy > EnemyAI: Attack Cooldown = 1.5, Attack Damage = 15.
Keep the cooldown longer than the attack clips, or the next trigger waits until the current clip ends.

MEDIUM
Replace attackDamage with:
    [SerializeField] private int[] attackDamages = { 10, 10, 20 };
Add a field to remember the attack that is playing:
    private int currentAttackIndex;
In StartAttack, before increasing attackIndex:
    currentAttackIndex = attackIndex;
In TryHitPlayer:
    playerHealth.TakeDamage(attackDamages[currentAttackIndex]);

CHALLENGE
In StartAttack replace the two attackIndex lines with:
    int randomIndex = Random.Range(0, AttackHashes.Length);
    animator.SetTrigger(AttackHashes[randomIndex]);
Then remove the attackIndex field.
