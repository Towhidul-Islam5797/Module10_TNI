SPRINT 13 EXERCISES

EASY
Add a new two-step recipe without writing code:
  1 Gem Ring + 1 Big Health Potion -> 1 "Royal Amulet" (stack 1, not usable).
Make the item (any icon), the recipe, and add it to the Player's recipe list.
Craft it in Play mode, then uncraft it. What do you get back?

MEDIUM
Show a hint while the panel is open: under the grid, a TMP text lists every recipe,
for example "Gold Bar = 4 Coin".
Hint: give CraftingGrid a public method that builds that text from the recipe list
(CraftingRecipe.DescribeIngredients already exists). CraftingPanelUI shows it in OpenPanel.

CHALLENGE
Count how many items the player has crafted and uncrafted, and show
"Crafted: 3  Uncrafted: 1" on the panel.
Rules: keep the counting in CraftingGrid (logic) and the text in CraftingPanelUI (UI).
Think about it: why should the numbers NOT go up when a craft fails because the inventory
is full?
