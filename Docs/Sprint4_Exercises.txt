SPRINT 4 EXERCISES

EASY
Make the enemy attack faster and hit harder: cooldown 1.5 s, damage 15. Inspector only.

MEDIUM
Make Kicking deal 20 damage while Boxing and Punching stay at 10.
Hint: an int array of damages in the same order as AttackHashes.

CHALLENGE
Pick a random attack each time instead of cycling in order.
Hint: Random.Range with an int max is exclusive.
