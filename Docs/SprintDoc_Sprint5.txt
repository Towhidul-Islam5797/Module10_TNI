SPRINT 5 - PLAYER DEATH (ThirdPerson scene)
Milestone 2: Combat

GOAL
When the player's health reaches 0, the player plays Dying, stays down and can no longer act.

FILES
New:
  Assets/Scripts/Combat/PlayerDeath.cs
    Every frame checks Health.IsDead. The first time it is true, Die():
      - turns off ThirdPersonController (move, jump, mouse look), PlayerCombat and PlayerInteractor
      - sets the UpperBody layer weight to 0 so a punch cannot freeze the arms
      - sets the Die trigger
      - turns itself off so Die() runs only once
Changed:
  Assets/Animations/ThirdPersonAnimator.controller
    New trigger Die. Base Layer: Any State -> Dying on Die.
    Can Transition To Self is off (Dying does not restart). Dying has no exit.
  Assets/Scenes/ThirdPerson.unity
    Player: added PlayerDeath.

DONE CONDITIONS
- At 0 health the player falls and stays on the ground.
- WASD, Space, mouse look, left/right click and E do nothing after death.
- The enemy goes back to patrolling.

KNOWN ISSUES
- Dying in the air freezes the player in the air, because gravity lives in ThirdPersonController.
- No restart yet. Stop and start Play mode to try again.

LEARN MORE
