SPRINT 7 - WORLD PICKUPS (ThirdPerson scene)
Milestone 3: Inventory Basics

GOAL
Items lie in the world. Looking at one shows "Press E to pick up <name>".
E puts it into the Inventory and removes it from the world.

HOW IT WORKS (in plain words)
- We reuse the interaction system from Module 10: PlayerInteractor shoots a ray
  from the camera. If the ray hits something with an IInteractable script, it shows
  that script's PromptText and calls its Interact method when E is pressed.
- ItemPickup is one more IInteractable, just like ColorBox and PickupBox.
  Its PromptText is "Press E to pick up " + the item name.
  Interact finds the Inventory on the player (same GameObject as PlayerInteractor)
  and calls AddItem.
    - Added: Destroy(gameObject), the item disappears.
    - Not added (inventory full): the item stays and "Inventory full" is logged.
- The pickups are simple shapes with their own URP Lit materials:
  coin = thin gold cylinder, potion = small red capsule, gem = blue cube on its corner.
  Each keeps the collider that comes with the shape, so the ray can hit it.

MANUAL PLAY-MODE TEST (teacher)
1. Press Play. Turn left and aim the crosshair at a gold coin.
   The prompt says "Press E to pick up Coin".
2. Press E. The coin disappears. Console: "Added Coin to empty slot 1".
3. Pick up a second coin. Console: "Added Coin to slot 1. Count: 2".
4. Pick up both potions and the gem. Each gets its own slot (potions stack together).
5. Select Player > Inventory > Slots in the Inspector: Coin 2, Health Potion 2, Gem 1.
6. Full test: in the Inspector set Slot Count to 2 before pressing Play.
   Pick up a coin and a potion, then look at the gem and press E:
   the gem stays and the Console says "Inventory full". Set Slot Count back to 6.
7. ColorBox and PickupBox still work with E.
