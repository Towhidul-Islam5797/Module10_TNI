SPRINT 4 - ENEMY ATTACK AND ENEMY DEATH (ThirdPerson scene)
Milestone 2: Combat

GOAL
The enemy stops near the player, faces them and cycles Boxing, Punching, Kicking.
Hits damage the player. At 0 health the enemy plays Dying and is removed.

FILES
Changed:
  Assets/Scripts/Enemy/EnemyAI.cs
    - New states: Attack and Dead.
    - Chase -> Attack when the player is within Attack Range (2 m).
      Attack -> Chase when the player is farther than Attack Range x 1.2 (the gap stops flickering between states).
      Any state -> Patrol when the player is dead.
    - Attack: stops the agent, turns to face the player, starts an attack every Attack Cooldown (2.5 s).
    - StartAttack sets the next trigger in the list (Boxing, Punching, Kicking) and uses
      Invoke to call TryHitPlayer after Hit Delay (0.5 s).
    - TryHitPlayer: damage only if the player is still in range and within Hit Angle (60 deg) in front.
      Uses distance and angle, not a raycast, because the Player is on the Ignore Raycast layer.
    - Die (runs once): stops pending hits, turns off the NavMeshAgent and collider,
      hides the health bar, plays Die, destroys the enemy after Destroy Delay (5 s).
    - Red gizmo sphere shows Attack Range.
  Assets/Animations/EnemyAnimator.controller
    Parameters: Speed, Boxing, Punching, Kicking, Die (triggers).
    Locomotion -> Boxing / Punching / Kicking on their trigger. Each returns to Locomotion at Exit Time 0.9.
    Kicking plays at Speed 1.5 (the clip is 3.4 s long).
    Any State -> Dying on Die. Can Transition To Self is off, so Dying does not restart. Dying has no exit.
  Assets/Scenes/ThirdPerson.unity
    Enemy > EnemyAI: new attack and death values, Health Bar = EnemyHealthCanvas.

DONE CONDITIONS
- The enemy chases, stops at about 1.5 m and attacks in the order Boxing, Punching, Kicking.
- Each landed hit lowers the green player bar by 10 and logs "Player took 10 damage".
- Stepping out of range during the 0.5 s before the hit avoids the damage.
- Killing the enemy plays Dying, hides its bar, stops it moving and attacking, and removes it after 5 s.

KNOWN ISSUES
- The player does not die yet. At 0 health the player keeps moving; the enemy goes back to patrol.
- Damage timing uses a fixed Hit Delay, not the exact frame of each clip.
