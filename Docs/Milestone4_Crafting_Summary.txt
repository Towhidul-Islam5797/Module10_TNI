MILESTONE 4 - CRAFTING SYSTEM: WORK SUMMARY
Project: Module10_TNI (Unity 6.3 LTS, URP, Input System)
Scene: ThirdPerson
Date: 2 Nov 2026

==================================================================
OVERVIEW
==================================================================
The ThirdPerson scene now has crafting:
- C or the "Craft (C)" button (bottom-right) opens a panel: 3x3 grid, two-way arrow, result.
- While it is open the cursor is free and movement, look, combat, E and item use are off.
- Drag items from the hotbar into the grid, one at a time. A matching recipe shows a
  half-transparent preview; dragging the preview to the hotbar crafts it.
- Drop a crafted item on the result slot to see its materials as previews; taking one
  material uncrafts it, dragging the item back cancels.
- Closing the panel returns everything to the inventory.
- Recipes: 4 Coin -> Gold Bar; 2 Health Potion + 1 Gem -> Big Health Potion (heals 60);
  1 Gold Bar + 1 Gem -> Gem Ring. 9 coins and 3 gems are in the world.

All new student scripts are in Unity tutorial style with explanatory comments: plain
methods, simple ifs, string names for input actions and Animator parameters, no lambdas,
LINQ, => members, nameof or singletons. The only interfaces used are Unity's drag-and-drop
interfaces, each explained in a comment.
