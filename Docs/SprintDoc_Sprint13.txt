SPRINT 13 - UNCRAFTING THROUGH THE UI, CLOSING, POLISH, LESSON (ThirdPerson scene)
Milestone 4: Crafting System

GOAL
Drop a crafted item on the result slot to take it apart: its materials appear as
half-transparent previews in the grid. Taking the first material finishes the uncraft.
Closing the panel gives back everything that is still in it. Write the lesson guide.

HOW IT WORKS (in plain words)
- Uncrafting is the same recipe read backwards. The logic was already written in Sprint 10
  (CraftingGrid). This sprint connects it to the UI with one change in CraftingSlotUI.OnDrop:
    hotbar box -> result box   CraftingGrid.MoveToResult(hotbar index)
  CraftingGrid then checks, in order:
    the grid must be empty      -> "Can't uncraft Gold Bar: empty the crafting grid first"
    a recipe must make the item -> "Can't uncraft Coin: no recipe makes it"
  If both are fine, the item leaves the hotbar and sits (real) on the result slot, and
  ShowIngredientPreviews puts one preview item per grid box, starting top-left.
- Finishing: drag any preview material to the hotbar (MoveGridToInventory -> CompleteUncraft).
  That material goes into the inventory, the crafted item is used up, the other previews become
  real items, and the player takes them one by one. If the inventory is full, nothing changes.
- Cancelling: drag the crafted item from the result box back to the hotbar
  (MoveResultToInventory -> CancelUncraft). It returns and the previews disappear.
- Closing the panel (C, the button, or dying) calls ReturnEverythingToInventory:
    real grid items       -> back to the inventory with AddItem
    an unfinished uncraft -> the crafted item goes back, previews are cleared
    a craft preview       -> just cleared (nothing was crafted)
    no room               -> the item stays in the grid; Console:
                             "Inventory full, some items stay in the crafting grid"
- Polish: the panel's hint line now also explains uncrafting.
- Lesson guide Docs/CraftingSystem_Lesson.md with full script listings (generated from the
  project files, so they match exactly), Editor steps, checkpoints, common mistakes, quiz
  and homework.

MANUAL PLAY-MODE TEST (teacher)
1. Press Play. Pick up all 9 coins, 3 gems and 2 potions. Press C.
2. Craft a Gold Bar (4 coins in the grid, drag the preview to the hotbar).
3. Drop the Gold Bar on the RESULT box: 4 faded coins fill the first 4 grid boxes.
   Console: "Uncrafting Gold Bar: ...".
4. Drag the Gold Bar from the result box back to the hotbar: "Uncraft cancelled...",
   the faded coins disappear.
5. Drop it on the result box again. Drag one faded coin to the hotbar:
   "Uncrafted Gold Bar into 4 Coin...". The result box is empty, 3 solid coins stay in the grid.
   Drag them to the hotbar one by one.
6. Drop a Coin on the result box: "Can't uncraft Coin: no recipe makes it". The coin stays.
7. Put a coin in the grid, then drop a Gold Bar on the result box:
   "Can't uncraft Gold Bar: empty the crafting grid first".
8. Gem Ring: craft a Gold Bar, then Gold Bar + Gem in the grid -> Gem Ring preview -> take it.
   Uncraft the Gem Ring: a faded Gold Bar and a faded Gem appear.
9. Big Health Potion: 2 potions + 1 gem. Take it. Close the panel, get hit by the enemy,
   select the Big Health Potion and press Q: the green bar goes up by 60.
10. Put 2 coins in the grid and close the panel with C: they are back in the hotbar.
11. Start an uncraft (Gold Bar on the result box) and close the panel: the Gold Bar is back.
12. Full inventory: fill all 6 hotbar boxes, put an item in the grid, fill the freed box, close:
    "Inventory full, some items stay in the crafting grid". Open again: the item is still there.
13. Console shows no errors. Combat, pickups, 1-6, Q, E and jumping all still work.
