SPRINT 9 EXERCISES

EASY
Make the Health Potion heal 40 instead of 25. Do not change any code.

MEDIUM
Select the next / previous slot with the mouse scroll wheel.
Hint: add an action "ScrollSlot" (Value, Axis) bound to <Mouse>/scroll/y.
Read it with ReadValue<float>(). Positive = previous slot, negative = next slot.
Wrap around: after slot 6 comes slot 1.

CHALLENGE
Add a drop key (G) that removes one of the selected item from the inventory
and puts a pickup for it back into the world in front of the player.
Hint: give ItemData a new field "worldPrefab" (GameObject). Make a prefab from
one coin, one potion and the gem, and assign them. Use Instantiate.
