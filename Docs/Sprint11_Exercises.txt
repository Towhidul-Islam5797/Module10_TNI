SPRINT 11 EXERCISES

EASY
Change how the panel looks without writing code:
- make the panel background dark blue instead of black
- make the title say "Workbench"
- move the Craft button to the top-right corner of the screen
Hint: CraftingPanel > Image > Color. For the button, use the anchor presets
(hold Shift+Alt) and change Pos Y.

MEDIUM
Close the panel with the Escape key too.
Hint: add an action "CloseMenu" bound to <Keyboard>/escape. In CraftingPanelUI, read it
like the toggle action, but only CLOSE the panel (never open it).
Note: in the Editor, Escape also frees the cursor of the Game view. Test it in a build,
or simply check that the panel closes.

CHALLENGE
Pause the world while the panel is open, so the enemy cannot hit you while you craft.
Use Time.timeScale = 0 when opening and 1 when closing.
Then answer: does dragging items still work with timeScale 0? Does the enemy's
NavMeshAgent stop? Does anything that uses Time.deltaTime still move?
