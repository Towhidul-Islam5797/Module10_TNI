MILESTONE 2 - COMBAT: WORK SUMMARY
Project: Module10_TNI (Unity 6.3 LTS, URP, Input System)
Scene: ThirdPerson
Date: 28 Oct 2026

==================================================================
OVERVIEW
==================================================================
The ThirdPerson scene now has a full melee combat loop:
- The player punches (left click) and kicks (right click) in combos.
- Punches work while walking or running. Kicks stop the player.
- Every attack fires a raycast that damages the enemy in front.
- The enemy patrols, chases, stops near the player and cycles three attacks.
- Both characters have health, a health bar and a death animation.

==================================================================
SPRINT 1 - HEALTH AND FIRST PUNCH
==================================================================
Added a shared Health component and the first player punch.
- Health.cs: max health, current health, TakeDamage, IsDead. Logs every hit to the Console.
- New layer "Enemy" (layer 8). The enemy got a Capsule Collider (it had none) and Health.
- First version used Animation Events and an AttackEventRelay. This was replaced in Sprint 2.

Problems found and fixed:
- The Attack trigger was missing from the Animator, causing "Parameter does not exist".
- The scene was never saved, so components were missing. Added them in the scene file.
- The code editor saved old copies over new scripts. Fix: close scripts in the editor before switching to Unity.

==================================================================
SPRINT 2 - PUNCH AND KICK COMBOS WITH RAYCAST DAMAGE
==================================================================
- Left click (Attack action): Punch -> Boxing -> Elbow Punch -> loop.
- Right click (new Kick action): Front Kick -> Kicking -> loop.
- Combos chain in the Animator: a click during a move starts the next one at Exit Time 0.5.
- Punches play on an UpperBody Animator layer with an Avatar Mask (torso, head, arms),
  so the legs keep running.
- Kicks play on the Base Layer (full body). The player cannot move while kicking.
- Damage: Physics.Raycast from chest height, 1.5 m forward, Enemy layer only.
  Punch 10, kick 20. Shared cooldown stops click spam.
- Debug.DrawRay shows the ray in the Scene view (green hit, red miss).
- Removed the HitFrame Animation Events and AttackEventRelay (damage is instant now).

Files: PlayerCombat.cs, ThirdPersonController.cs, InputSystem_Actions.inputactions,
       ThirdPersonAnimator.controller, UpperBodyMask.mask (new), fight clip .meta files.

==================================================================
SPRINT 3 - HEALTH BARS
==================================================================
- Player bar: green, bottom left of the HUD.
- Enemy bar: red, floating 2.2 m above the enemy on a World Space canvas, always facing the camera.
- HealthBar.cs: sets a Filled Image's fillAmount to CurrentHealth / MaxHealth every frame.
- FaceCamera.cs: turns the bar to face the camera in LateUpdate.
- Health.cs: added MaxHealth.

Problems found and fixed:
- Unity saved an old copy of the scene over the new one, so the bars vanished. Re-added them.
- Arms showed a T-pose when not punching. The UpperBody layer now starts at weight 0,
  and PlayerCombat fades it in only while a punch plays.

==================================================================
SPRINT 4 - ENEMY ATTACK AND ENEMY DEATH
==================================================================
- EnemyAI.cs got two new states: Attack and Dead.
- Within 2 m the enemy stops, faces the player and attacks every 2.5 s,
  cycling Boxing -> Punching -> Kicking.
- The hit lands 0.5 s after the attack starts (Invoke). It only hurts if the player is still
  in range and in front (distance and angle check, because the Player is on Ignore Raycast).
- When the player is dead, the enemy goes back to patrolling.
- Enemy death: plays Dying, turns off the NavMeshAgent and collider, hides its health bar,
  is destroyed after 5 s.
- EnemyAnimator.controller: Boxing, Punching, Kicking and Die triggers, Any State -> Dying.

Problems found and fixed:
- Animations drifted, snapped back and the dead body floated. Cause: root motion is off and the
  clips had Bake Into Pose off; Dying had no import settings at all.
  Fix on all six fight clips: Bake Into Pose ON (Rotation, Y, XZ), Based Upon = Original.
  Dying set to frames 0-130, Loop Time off.

==================================================================
SPRINT 5 - PLAYER DEATH
==================================================================
- PlayerDeath.cs (new, written in tutorial style with student comments):
  at 0 health it turns off movement, combat and interaction, hides the punch layer,
  plays Die and turns itself off.
- ThirdPersonAnimator.controller: Die trigger, Any State -> Dying.
