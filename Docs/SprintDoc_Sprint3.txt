SPRINT 3 - HEALTH BARS (ThirdPerson scene)
Milestone 2: Combat

GOAL
Show player health on the HUD and enemy health above the enemy's head.

FILES
New (Assets/Scripts/UI/):
  HealthBar.cs    Every frame sets a fill Image to CurrentHealth / MaxHealth (1 = full, 0 = empty).
  FaceCamera.cs   In LateUpdate turns the object to face the same way as the main camera.
Changed:
  Assets/Scripts/Combat/Health.cs   Added read-only MaxHealth.
  Assets/Scenes/ThirdPerson.unity
    Player: added Health (100).
    HUDCanvas > PlayerHealthBar (dark background, bottom left, 400 x 30) > Fill (green).
      PlayerHealthBar has HealthBar: Health = Player, Fill Image = Fill.
    Enemy > EnemyHealthCanvas (World Space canvas, 2.2 m up, scale 0.01) > Background > Fill (red).
      EnemyHealthCanvas has HealthBar (Health = Enemy) and FaceCamera.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Fill images use Image Type = Filled, Fill Method = Horizontal, Fill Origin = Left.
   fillAmount 0.5 shows half the bar.
2. HealthBar divides with (float) so 90 / 100 gives 0.9, not 0 (whole-number division).
3. A World Space canvas lives in the 3D world like any object. Scale 0.01 turns 100 x 12 UI units into 1 m x 0.12 m.
4. FaceCamera runs in LateUpdate, after the camera has moved, so the bar never lags a frame behind.

DONE CONDITIONS
- The enemy bar floats above its head and stays readable from any camera angle.
- Each punch shrinks the enemy bar by 10%, each kick by 20%.
- The player bar shows full at the bottom left.

KNOWN ISSUES
- Nothing damages the player yet, so the player bar stays full until the enemy attack sprint.
- The enemy bar stays visible at 0 health until the death sprint.
